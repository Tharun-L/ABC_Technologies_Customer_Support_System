FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
ABC Technologies

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered
email address, and a password reset link will be sent. The link expires
after 1 hour. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or
contact support to verify your account email is correct.

Q: How do I update my profile information?
A: Log in and go to Account Settings > Profile. You can update your name,
email, phone number, and company details there. Email changes require
verification via a confirmation link sent to the new address.

Q: How do I activate or deactivate my account?
A: New accounts are activated automatically after email verification during
signup. To deactivate an account temporarily, go to Account Settings >
Manage Subscription > Deactivate Account. Deactivation pauses billing but
retains your data for 90 days.

Q: How do I request a refund?
A: Refund requests can be submitted through Billing > Request Refund, or by
contacting support directly. All refund requests are reviewed by a human
supervisor per company policy before being processed.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Account Settings > Manage Subscription > Cancel Subscription.
Annual plan cancellations require supervisor confirmation. Monthly plans
cancel at the end of the current billing period.

Q: Why did my application crash when uploading a file?
A: This is usually caused by file size limits, unsupported formats, or
browser issues. See the Technical Manual for detailed troubleshooting steps.

Q: What are your pricing plans?
A: We offer Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans. See the Pricing
Guide for full details on features and costs.

Q: How can I escalate an issue to management?
A: Let your support agent know you'd like to escalate, or mention it in your
message. Escalation requests are always routed to a human supervisor for
personal follow-up.

Q: Can I get compensation for a service outage?
A: Compensation requests (credits, discounts, extended trials) are evaluated
case-by-case and require human supervisor approval.
