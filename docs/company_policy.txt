COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT
ABC Technologies

1. REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase for annual subscriptions,
provided the account has been used for fewer than 10 hours of active product usage.
Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable after 7 days. All refund requests must be
reviewed and approved by a human supervisor before processing. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval.

2. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers can request cancellation of their subscription at any time. Cancellation
requests for annual plans require human supervisor approval to confirm the customer
understands any applicable early-termination terms. Monthly plans can be cancelled
at the end of the current billing cycle without supervisor approval, but cancellation
confirmation emails are always reviewed before sending.

3. ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY
Permanent account closure requests must be approved by a human supervisor. Before
approval, the supervisor must confirm there is no outstanding balance and that the
customer has been informed that all data will be deleted after 30 days.

4. COMPENSATION POLICY
Any compensation offered to a customer (service credits, extended trial periods,
discount codes) for service disruptions or dissatisfaction must be approved by a
human supervisor. Support agents are not authorized to offer compensation directly.

5. ESCALATION TO MANAGEMENT POLICY
If a customer explicitly requests to speak with management or escalates a complaint
beyond standard support, this must be routed to a human supervisor for review and
personal follow-up. No automated response should be sent as the final resolution
for management escalations.

6. DATA PRIVACY POLICY
Customer data is never shared with third parties without consent. Support agents
must verify customer identity (name and account email) before discussing billing
or account details.

7. RESPONSE TIME COMMITMENTS
- Sales inquiries: response within 4 business hours.
- Technical support: response within 2 business hours for critical issues.
- Billing inquiries: response within 1 business day.
- Account management: response within 1 business day.
